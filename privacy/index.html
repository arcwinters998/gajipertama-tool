Privacy

GajiPertama Content Manager is a private tool for one person: the owner of the TikTok channel @gajipertama.id.

What it handles

  • The TikTok login token. This is the key that lets the tool upload to the owner's own account. It is created when the owner signs in and gives their permission.
  • The owner's own videos and the captions for them.

Where it is stored

Everything stays on the owner's own Windows PC, in a folder on their own computer. Nothing is sent to a server of ours, because there is no such server. There is no online database and no cloud account.

What is not collected

No information about other people. No viewer data, no follower lists, no passwords, no payment details, no advertising data.

Sharing

Nothing is passed on to third parties. Nothing is sold. No data is sent to anyone else.

Taking permission back

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time in their TikTok settings. After that the saved login token stops working and the tool cannot upload any more. They can also delete the files on the PC whenever they want.

Data retention

The login token is kept only on the owner's own PC, and only for as long as the permission is given. If the owner removes the tool's access in their TikTok settings, the token stops working and can be deleted from the PC. The videos and captions stay on the PC until the owner deletes them. There is no copy anywhere else.

Children

This tool is not for children and not for anyone under 13. It is used only by the owner, who is an adult. The channel speaks to young adults who have started working. The tool does not collect any information about children or about other people.

Changes

If something about the tool changes, this page is updated to match. The owner can also stop using the tool at any time.
