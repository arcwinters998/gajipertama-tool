GajiPertama Content Manager

GajiPertama Content Manager is a small, private tool built for one person: the owner of the TikTok

channel @gajipertama.id.

About the channel

@gajipertama.id posts short money tips and motivation in Indonesian. The videos are made for young

people who have just started their first job — how to split a first salary, how to build a small

emergency fund, how to stay calm about money. Every video is written and made by the owner. The tool

only helps with the uploading. It is not a public service: there is no sign-up, no other users and no

content from anyone else.

How the tool works

1. A video is made. The owner writes the script and makes the video on their own computer.

2. Preview. The owner opens the tool and sees a preview: which video is next, the caption, and

where it will go.

3. The owner confirms. Nothing happens until the owner says yes. The tool never posts on its own.

4. Upload. After that confirmation the tool sends the video to the owner's own TikTok account —

either into the TikTok inbox, where the owner taps "Post" themselves, or as a direct post, if the

owner chooses that.

5. Status. The tool then shows the state of the upload, so the owner can see what is already

online and what is still waiting.

TikTok permissions used

  • user.info.basic — to show the owner the name of the connected TikTok account, so they can check

that it is the right one.

  • video.publish — to publish a video to the account, but only after the owner has confirmed it.
  • video.upload — to send a video into the TikTok inbox as a draft, so the owner can finish and

post it inside TikTok.

No other permissions are requested.

Data & Privacy

Everything stays on the owner's own Windows PC. The tool handles the owner's own videos, their

captions, and the TikTok login token. Nothing is sent to a server of ours — there is no such server.

No information about other people is collected, and nothing is shared with third parties. The owner

can remove the tool's access at any time in their TikTok settings.
